## DESCRIPTION  (4000 characters max)

```
Symponia is a space to think.

Not a feed. Not a coach barking habits at you. A companion that asks the question
underneath the question, and then stays quiet long enough for you to answer it.

Most apps want your attention. This one wants your attention back.


HOW IT WORKS

You begin by answering a few honest questions, and by choosing seven animals —
your archetypes. The one you pick first becomes your dominant. The one you pick
last becomes your shadow: the part of yourself you tend to look away from.

From there, Symponia already knows something about how to speak to you. It doesn't
quote your answers back at you like a form you filled in. It simply arrives
knowing.

Then you talk. About today, about the thing you keep circling, about whatever is
actually there.


THREE WAYS IN

ARCHETYPE — meet your animals. What each one gives you, what it costs you, and
what it's asking of you.

MY DAY — a reflection shaped by today, and by how you're actually feeling.

CONVERSATION — open. Bring anything.


HOW YOU'RE DOING, NOT HOW OFTEN YOU SHOW UP

Symponia asks how you're feeling when you arrive, and again after you've talked.
Over a week you see the difference between the two.

This deliberately replaces the streak. A streak measures how obedient you've been.
This measures whether the thing is working — which is the only question that
matters, and the only one most apps are careful never to ask.

If there isn't enough there to say something honest, it says nothing. It will not
draw you a confident line through two points.


IT SPEAKS YOUR LANGUAGE

English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian, Danish, Swedish.

Not machine-translated. Written to read as though it were composed in your
language — down to the daily reflection that arrives each morning.


PRIVACY

Your conversations are yours.

Memory is off by default. If you turn it on, Symponia remembers between sessions;
if you leave it off, nothing is kept. Mood check-ins are optional, and can always
be dismissed — a question you can't decline isn't a question.

You can delete everything, at any time, from Settings.


WHAT IT IS NOT

Symponia is not therapy, and it will not pretend to be. It does not diagnose, it
does not treat, and it will not tell you what to do with your life.

It is a mirror that talks back. That turns out to be enough, more often than
you'd think.


SYMPONIA PREMIUM

Seven days free, then £19.99/month.

Unlimited reflection — no counters, no balance, nothing to ration. Fair-use limits
apply to protect the service; they sit far above ordinary use and you are unlikely
to ever meet them.

Cancel any time in your App Store settings.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription
renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the
end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours
prior to the end of the current period. You can manage and cancel your
subscription in your App Store account settings.

Terms: https://symponia.io/terms
Privacy: https://symponia.io/privacy
```

## KEYWORDS  (100 characters max, comma-separated, NO spaces after commas)

Do not repeat words already in your app name or subtitle — Apple indexes those
anyway, and repeating them wastes the field.

```
journal,reflect,shadow,jung,archetype,mindful,selfcare,therapy,anxiety,mood,diary,calm,think,ai
```
(94)

A note on `therapy`: it's a high-traffic keyword and it is legitimate to be found
by it, but your description must never *claim* to be therapy — and it doesn't. Keep
it that way; the claim is what gets rejected, not the keyword.

## WHAT TO WATCH FOR

Apple reads the description as a promise. Two lines in there are doing real work
to keep you safe:

- **"Symponia is not therapy, and it will not pretend to be."** Health-adjacent AI
  apps get scrutinised hard. Saying this plainly, in the listing itself, is worth
  more than any wording you could hide behind.

- **The subscription paragraph** is the standard Apple-required auto-renewal
  disclosure, close to verbatim. Do not trim it. Reviewers look for it.

And the thing that will actually get you rejected if you forget it: the price in
this description (£19.99, 7 days free) must MATCH what's in App Store Connect and
what's written at symponia.io/terms. Right now the terms page still says £12.99 /
350 sessions. Fix that before you submit or none of this copy will save you.
