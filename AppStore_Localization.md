# Symponia — App Store listing, localized (9 languages)

Paste each block into App Store Connect → **App Store** tab → add the language under
*Localizable Information*, then fill the matching fields.

**Apple limits:** Name 30 · Subtitle 30 · Promotional Text 170 · Keywords 100 (comma-separated,
**no spaces after commas** — spaces waste characters) · Description 4000.

**Compliance rules baked into every version below:** no clinical claims (no "treats anxiety",
"therapy", "cure", "diagnosis"). Positioned as reflection and self-understanding, matching the
in-app "not a therapist" framing and the reviewer notes. Keep it that way in any edits.

**Positioning:** lead with the shadow/animal hook (the shareable mechanic), pay off with the
relational benefit (how you love, argue, work) — that's the stronger motivator than abstract
self-knowledge.

## Notes before you publish

1. **Screenshots per language.** Apple lets you upload different screenshots per locale. Your current shots have English captions burned in. Minimum viable: keep the English screenshots everywhere for now (allowed), then localize captions for Russian/German/Spanish first — those are the biggest wins.
2. **Don't machine-translate the keywords later.** Keywords are search behaviour, not translation — "shadow work" in Russian is what Russians *type*, not a literal rendering. The ones above are chosen as search terms, not translations.
3. **No clinical claims anywhere.** Every description above avoids "therapy", "treat", "cure", "anxiety". Keep it that way — that's the line that gets wellness apps rejected.
4. **Add the languages to your app's supported locales** in App Store Connect so the store page shows them.
